        New part

        Pick a package, check its dimensions (millimetres, from the datasheet drawing) and give the part a name. The pins start as PIN1, PIN2, …; name them in the pin table, or drag a box over the pin table of the datasheet.

        Package dimensions

        New part from a datasheet

        A language model reads the text of the datasheet and fills in the pins and the package. Every pin is checked against the page text, and you review each stage. This sends text of the datasheet to the models in your kirpitch.toml; the result says which of them leave this machine. Scanned PDFs (no text) do not work.

        Reading the datasheet… this can take a minute or more.
