    1 · Pinout

    Check every pin against the datasheet: its number, its name and its electrical type. Click a pin to see it in the drawings and on the datasheet page. Edit a cell to fix it.

    2 · Footprint

    Check the package dimensions and the pads (millimetres, y points down). Pin 1 has a bold outline. Click a pad to find it on the datasheet.

    3 · 3D model

    The model is built from the package and the pads. Check that the body, the pin 1 mark and the terminals sit where they should.

    Dark is the body, light grey the pin 1 mark, silver the terminals; the green line is the board.
